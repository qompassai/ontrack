# TRIS submission materials — ONTrack

This folder holds the TDS TRIS (Technology Request Information Sheet) submission materials for **ONTrack** (`ai.qompass.ontrack`), prepared 2026-11-11. ONTrack was submitted together with LightShow, Fathom, Versemend, the Salesforce MCP integration, and Niflheim in one combined email to DLT-IT TRIS Request; the combined package (the email text, the full six-product worksheet, and the source form/process documents) is kept in the requestor's submission records and is not published here.

## Contents

- [WORKSHEET.md](WORKSHEET.md) — every requestor-facing TRIS form field for ONTrack, pre-filled from the submission evidence; open items are marked **[INPUT NEEDED]**.
- [CHECKLIST.md](CHECKLIST.md) — one line per TRIS requirement: where the answer is entered (form section and row, and the process step at which it is submitted) and its status, with links to the satisfying evidence in this folder.
- [BUSINESS-USE.md](BUSINESS-USE.md) — the business case at TDS, including the data-flow disclosure (ONTrack is networked by default; stop addresses and coordinates go to Nominatim and OSRM, with an ip-api.com fallback).
